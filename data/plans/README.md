# Plans and drawings

**What goes here:** floor plans, sections, elevations and site drawings we are given or find for a specific building (from Payette, Northeastern Facilities, the City of Boston, the Archives).

**Rule:** the files are not committed by default, because most are the owner's property. Before any drawing is committed, someone with the right to share it should say so, and the permission goes in the building's README. Name files `<building-code>_<what>_<year>.<ext>` (for example `isec_section-A_2016.pdf`).
