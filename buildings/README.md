## Pilot candidate
**Richards Hall** (`RI`, 360 Huntington Avenue): built 1938, basement plus 5 levels above grade, 113,827 GSF (matrix). The DRS photo index (`data/tables/drs_photo_index.csv`) has about 25 view-type hits and 6 drawing hits for it, including scanned floor plans for the basement and levels 1 to 4. The Archives also hold an "Architectural Drawings > Richards Hall" folder. I have not opened the floor-plan images, so whether they have a scale or dimensions is unknown, and the matrix's 5 levels above grade against 4 plans needs checking.

Second choice: **Dodge Hall** (`DG`), 1952, basement plus 4 levels plus penthouse, 85,826 GSF. "Dodge Library" in the Archives is the same building (a DRS item is titled "Architectural drawing of Dodge Hall (Dodge Library)"). About 15 view hits and 2 drawing hits.

Index coverage is uneven: 46 of the 98 Boston buildings have at least one hit under their matrix name. "No hits" can mean a different name in the archive (for example "Egan Center" in the matrix versus "Egan Engineering and Science Research Center" in the Archives), so aliases are the next fix. Those buildings are the ones to photograph first.
